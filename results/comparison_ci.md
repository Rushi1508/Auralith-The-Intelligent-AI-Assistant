Two of baseline's correct answers are not grounded: on bert-001 and bert-005 the
model says the text does not contain the answer and then answers from its own
memory. The judge checks correctness, not grounding, so both count. Without them
baseline is 11/28 by hand. Separately, on unans-002 baseline refuses in prose
instead of with `NOT_IN_CONTEXT`. The refusal is right in substance but breaks
the output contract, so it is scored wrong.

## What this changes

- **The retrieval gains are real.** Every retrieval metric's interval excludes
  zero, including MRR, which does not depend on k.
- **Tuned retrieval did not measurably improve answers.** With the fixed judge,
  baseline and tuned tie at 14/28. The earlier +0.107 (10/28 → 13/28) came from
  the v1 judge's false negatives on baseline (attn-001, attn-002, attn-009,
  rag-004). The tie hides a lot of movement. By hand, tuned is right on 8
  questions baseline gets wrong, and wrong on 6 that baseline gets right. Three
  of the wins come from retrieval: attn-004, attn-007 and rag-003 had no
  relevant page at baseline. The losses are all generator mistakes with the
  right page in context. attn-003 takes BERT's head count. bert-001 gives
  BERT-LARGE the wrong hidden size. bert-005 and xdoc-001 leave out the
  requested names. rag-004 refuses. rag-007 cites passages but says nothing.
- **The partial-answer prompt is the best run on every measure, but not
  significantly.** The judge's +0.071 and the hand-scored +0.143 both have
  intervals that reach zero, and so does the drop in wrong refusals from 5/26
  to 2/26.
- **abstention_accuracy on n=2 carries no information**, as already stated.

Retrieval changes are detectable on this question set. End-to-end changes
under about 0.15 are not. The generator is now the bottleneck, and showing a
generation-side improvement needs either a large effect or more questions.

qwen2.5:7b is also the judge model, so every qwen answer was read by hand.
Hand 22/28 matches the judge's 22/28, with two errors in each direction
(table above), so there is no sign that the judge favours its own model's answers.

On hand scores the gain is smaller than the judge reports, because the judge
under-scores the 3B run (16 vs 19 by hand):

| comparison | hand delta | 95% CI | |
|---|---|---|---|
| partial (3B) → qwen (7B) | +3/28 (+0.107) | [-0.036, +0.250] | includes 0 |

By hand the 7B model wins on bert-003, bert-005, bert-008 and rag-008, which
are all multi-part answers that the 3B model left incomplete. It loses on
attn-007, where it refuses with the answer in context. It still refuses wrongly on
attn-006 and bert-006, gets attn-008 wrong (it gives the big model's 3.5 days as
the base model's training time), and on xdoc-001 it does not name the paper.
Borderline passes: attn-005 leaves out the softmax-gradient reason, and rag-001
expands RAG as "Research Augmented Generation".
